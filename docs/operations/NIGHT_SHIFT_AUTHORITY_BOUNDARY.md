# Night Shift v0 Authority Boundary

Night Shift v0 unattended operation is limited to qualified T0/T1 LOW-risk work using one logical dispatcher and one worker. Local qualified model execution is permitted only inside the frozen isolated workspace and authorized mutation scope.

Successful work may pass deterministic verification, controlled L1 canonical integration, and explicitly authorized L2 local commit promotion.

Night Shift v0 has no unattended authority to push, deploy, merge, rebase, amend, modify remotes, mutate infrastructure, perform external side effects, or invoke Codex. Unsupported capability or authority requirements must fail closed or surface as escalation.
