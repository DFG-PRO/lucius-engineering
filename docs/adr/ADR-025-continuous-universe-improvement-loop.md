# ADR-025: Continuous Universe Improvement Loop

**Status:** Accepted
**Phase:** 1.8

## Context

The DFG Universe needs a durable mechanism for improving systems from operational
reality rather than from isolated, one-time planning.

## Decision

Alfred coordinates periodic and event-triggered improvement reviews across the
DFG Universe.

Inputs include:

- operational metrics
- failures
- technical debt
- documentation drift
- architecture drift
- learning candidates
- research opportunities
- cost/performance trends

Roles:

- Darwin: external research, technology opportunities, benchmarks
- Lucius: engineering analysis, architecture, technical debt, implementation proposals
- Specialized engines: operational evidence, domain results, constraints
- Alfred: coordination, prioritization, work package creation
- Daniel: ultimate authority for important decisions

Cadences:

- CONTINUOUS
- MONTHLY operational improvement review
- QUARTERLY architecture/strategy review
- EVENT-TRIGGERED review

Components may self-analyze, self-propose, and self-test, but important decisions
remain subject to Daniel's authority.

## Consequences

Improvement work becomes an explicit, reviewable loop. Lucius contributes
engineering judgment and implementation proposals without becoming the sole
planner or ultimate authority.
